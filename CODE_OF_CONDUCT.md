# Code of conduct

LanTally contributors are expected to keep technical discussion respectful, specific, and evidence-based.

- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome contributors regardless of experience or background.
- Do not publish private network data, credentials, or identifying traffic records.
- Do not use project channels for harassment, threats, discrimination, or unwanted personal attention.
- Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or users.

Concerns should be reported privately to the maintainers. A public moderation contact will be added before the first release.
